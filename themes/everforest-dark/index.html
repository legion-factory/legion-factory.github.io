Where the palette comes from

Everforest is a color scheme by sainnhe, first published on GitHub in 2019 as a Vim color scheme under the MIT license. The author lists several projects among its inspirations, including vim-color-spring-night, sacredforest-vim, gruvbox and Solarized.

In the author’s words, Everforest is “a green based color scheme; it's designed to be warm and soft in order to protect developers' eyes.” It comes in dark and light versions, each with hard, medium and soft contrast settings.

Why people use it

The project’s README highlights a green-based but warm-toned palette, soft contrast, and good behavior together with night-light tools such as redshift and f.lux. The repository has more than 4,000 stars on GitHub, and a project wiki collects related ports to other applications.

The author presents Everforest alongside the other color schemes he maintains — Gruvbox Material, Edge and Sonokai — mostly through GitHub READMEs, screenshots of each contrast variant and detailed documentation.
