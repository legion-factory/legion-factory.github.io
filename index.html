Page Link Status - Broken Link Checker v0.3.17

Check every link on the page you are viewing and see which ones work, redirect, or are broken.

  • One-click scan of all http(s) links on the current tab
  • Statuses: OK, Redirect (with final URL), Broken, Manual, Skip
  • Links outlined in color directly on the page
  • Export results to CSV
  • Runs only when you click Scan; nothing is sent to us
