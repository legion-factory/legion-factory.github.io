Find 1-2 star App Store reviews of an iOS app

Low-star reviews are the ones a product team usually wants first. This run keeps ratings of one or two stars and looks in more than one country store.

Run this example on Apify

Open the app-store-reviews actor in the Apify Store

Price: $0.10 per 1,000 reviews. Error rows are not charged. Actor start is $0.00005.

Input for this example

{
  "startUrls": [
    "https://apps.apple.com/us/app/spotify-music-and-podcasts/id324684580"
  ],
  "countries": [
    "us",
    "gb"
  ],
  "minRating": 1,
  "maxRating": 2,
  "maxReviewsPerApp": 20
}

What this example does not do

Reviews of three stars and above are excluded. This is not a sentiment model; it only filters on the star rating the reviewer chose.
