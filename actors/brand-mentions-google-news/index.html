$ legion actor brand-mentions-google-news

Monitor brand mentions in Google News

A quoted query such as “Tesla” is a simple way to collect the day’s headlines that mention a brand, ready to paste into a sheet or a daily digest.

Run this example on Apify

Open the google-news actor in the Apify Store

Price: $1.00 per 1,000 articles. Plus $0.50 per 1,000 resolved publisher URLs. Failed decodes and error rows are not charged. Actor start is $0.00005.

Input for this example

{
  "queries": [
    "\"Tesla\""
  ],
  "timeRange": "1d",
  "maxArticlesPerQuery": 10,
  "resolveUrls": true,
  "maxUrlResolves": 10
}
