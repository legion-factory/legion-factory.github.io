$ legion actor google-news-real-article-urls

Get Google News articles with real publisher URLs

Google News RSS links go through news.google.com. This run asks the actor to decode those wrappers so the table contains the publisher URL you can open or archive.

Run this example on Apify

Open the google-news actor in the Apify Store

Price: $1.00 per 1,000 articles. Plus $0.50 per 1,000 resolved publisher URLs. Failed decodes and error rows are not charged. Actor start is $0.00005.
