Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Legion Labs builds privacy-first software. This policy explains what data our extensions and website handle.

Extensions process data locally

  • Page Link Status reads link addresses and text on a tab only when you click Scan, and requests those links directly from your browser. Results are kept in your browser's session storage. The checked sites see these requests as normal browser traffic (including your IP address). Requests are sent without cookies, with one exception: if a link on the same site as the scanned page (same registrable domain) answers 401, 403 or 404 to the anonymous check, it is rechecked once with your own browser session (your cookies for that site), so pages that need your sign-in are not reported as broken. Cookies are never sent to other sites. You can turn this off in Options with “Recheck same-site links with my session (if anonymous check fails)” (on by default). No cookies or page data leave your browser to us; nothing is sent to us.
  • Site Font Switcher (coming soon) stores your per-site font rules in your browser's local storage and changes page styles locally. Nothing is sent to us.

Our extensions contain no analytics, tracking, advertising, or remote code, and we do not sell or share personal data.

Purchases

When you buy a Pro license, Lemon Squeezy (our merchant of record) processes your name, email, billing and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email, order details and license key information to provide the license and support. We do not receive your full card details.

Website

This website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies or analytics. Your light/dark theme choice is stored only in your browser's localStorage and is never sent anywhere. GitHub may log technical request data per its own privacy statement.

Email

If you email us, we use your message only to respond and keep it no longer than needed.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete data we hold about you (e.g., support emails, order records except where law requires keeping them) at ppo.viktorov.aa@gmail.com.
